Everything for the shoot on one page, in order. Details live in [VIDEO.md](VIDEO.md) and [DEMO_SCRIPT.md](DEMO_SCRIPT.md).

## 1. Set up (about 10 minutes)

- [ ] **Reset the playbook.** qcloser.vercel.app/admin, paste the admin key (the `ADMIN_KEY` line in `web/.env.local`), Reset to seed.
- [ ] **Reload Q** in Chrome: chrome://extensions, the reload arrow on Q.
- [ ] **Q popup on the laptop:** name `Jordan`, city `Long Island`, call length `10 min`, share `Everyone`, then paste the two blocks below.
- [ ] **Second Q card:** a second Chrome profile with Q loaded, name `Alex` (anything but Jordan), with meet.google.com open and visible on screen.
- [ ] **Recorder:** OBS, or Loom with computer audio on. Record a 20-second test with the helper talking and play it back.
- [ ] **Earbuds** on the laptop, **Do Not Disturb** on, extra tabs and the bookmarks bar hidden.
- [ ] **Helper** goes to another room and joins the Meet on their phone with the display name `John`.
- [ ] **Captions:** press C on the laptop, English.
- [ ] **Rehearse once** with the helper, then End call → **Lost**. Then reset again only if the rehearsal clicked Won.

## 2. The live call (start recording first)

**Send this to your helper.** Say each line in one breath, and wait for the seller to answer before the next one.

```text
1. Sell me this pen.
2. I run a brokerage. I don't need a pen. I've got a drawer full of them.
3. A ten-year lease on our new office. I sign it Friday.
4. Forty bucks for a pen? I get twenty for five dollars.
5. Look, it's a nice pen. Let me think about it.
6. (pause) Because it's the signature people remember. It should feel like the deal. Fine. Can you get it to me before Friday?
7. J. B.
```

**You (the seller):** glance at the card after each line and act on the nudge in your own words. Rough guide:

| After John says | You say |
|---|---|
| "Sell me this pen." | "Sure. First, what do you sign in a normal week?" |
| "…drawer full of them." | "Fair. So which pen closes your deals? What's the next big one you're signing?" |
| "…I sign it Friday." | "Then this is your Friday pen." (or act on the nudge) |
| "Forty bucks for a pen?…" | "You can. But it's forty bucks against a ten-year lease." |
| "Let me think about it." | **Ignore the nudge and flip it:** "Let me flip it. You sell it to me. Why would you sign a ten-year lease with a two-dollar pen?" |
| "…before Friday?" | "Thursday, engraved. What initials?" |
| "J. B." | "Done. I'm sending the link now." |

📸 **Gallery image 1:** when a nudge lands (ideally one with the buyer tag), press Cmd+Shift+4, then Space, and click the Chrome window. Keep the meet.google.com address bar and John's video tile in the shot.

## 3. Right after the call (keep recording)

1. Click **End call** on the card, then **Won**.
2. Wait for your toast: "Q learned a new move from you: …" 📸
3. Bring the second Chrome window on screen. The toast "New move learned from Jordan · Long Island" lands within a few seconds. 📸
4. Back on your card, the result toast ("X of Y nudges helped"), then click **See the call report** and scroll slowly.
5. Open **/playbook**: the new move with the **New** chip, "What Q learned", "What Q got wrong".

If most nudges went unused or a line got fumbled, reset at /admin and reshoot the call.

## 5. Narration (record all of it in one go)

Quiet room, phone voice memo a hand's width from your mouth.

```text
Every closer needs a Q.

Q listens to your sales call and shows you, at a glance, what the best salespeople would do next. Every win teaches it something new.

AI that feeds you lines is a forklift at the gym: the weight goes up, you never get stronger.

When you win, Q finds the move you improvised and credits it to you.

Every other Q card has it in seconds.

Then Q grades its own nudges by what the buyer did next. The ones that keep hurting drop, then retire.

Lose, and Q keeps the lesson.

This is closing intelligence: about twelve cents a call on our standard model, against a $39 to $99 seat.

A hundred thousand seats is $100 million a year: a billion-dollar company at ten times revenue.

Anyone can call the same model. Nobody can copy which moves actually closed.

Claude reads the call, my company profile and the clock, then fits a move from the playbook to this buyer.

Every closer needs a Q.
```
